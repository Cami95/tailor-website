Cynthia Shambley has designed and sewn professionally for over 45 years. Early on, her talent for creativity and eye for detail in her fashions awarded her titles of "best dressed" but also a business acumen in her sewing prom dresses for her friends, uniforms for a Gospel Choir and uniforms for the High School Pom-pom Team. She went on to designing and sewing wedding and bridesmaid dresses, tuxedos, pageant wear, costumes and custom apparel for women, men, and children. Before branding was a business strategy, she wanted a name for her business that would capture the essence of her style and designs. She has been featured as a designer and seamstress and has showcased her awe-inspiring fashions in Ohio, Illinois and the Washington DC area. She is now making her collections available to the public for purchase as well as her signature "Sham Shawl," a shawl with sleeves.
