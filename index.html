Designer Cynthia Shambley

Cynthia Shambley is a fashion designer, seamstress, and artist who specializes in creating custom-made fashions for the style-conscious consumer who seeks a trend-setting, Sewphisticated, quality constructed and functional wardrobe. Her inspiration came from her parents who were both artists. She inherited this artistic talent as well as she apprenticed under her fashion designer and seamstress mother as well as her two older sisters. The name "Sunday Best" was divinely inspired and is not only historical, cultural, but also inspirational. "Sunday Best" is defined as one's best clothing worn to Church or on special occasions. That is how she describes her clothing, unique, versatile, trendy, expertly sewn, and the best garments in your closet for your special occasions. She is now making her collections available to the public for purchase as well as her signature "Sham Shawl," a shawl with sleeves.
